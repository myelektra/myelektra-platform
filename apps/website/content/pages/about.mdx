# About Myelektra

We are an AI-powered revenue growth partner helping B2B companies move from scattered prospecting to structured, measurable revenue systems.

## Our Story

Built for Companies That Need More Than Contact Data.

Myelektra was founded with a clear observation: most B2B companies struggle not because they lack data, but because they lack a system. They have spreadsheets of contacts, lists of leads, and CRM databases — but no structured process to turn those into revenue.

We built Myelektra to solve this problem. Our approach combines AI-powered insights with human expertise to create revenue systems that are measurable, scalable, and sustainable.

## Our Mission

To help B2B companies build predictable revenue systems that combine technology, process, and people.
