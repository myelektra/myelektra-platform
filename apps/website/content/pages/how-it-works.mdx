A structured, eight-step process that takes you from discovery to a measurable and continuously improving revenue pipeline.

2. **Market Research** — Analyze your target market and competition
3. **ICP Development** — Define your ideal customer profile
4. **Buyer Persona Mapping** — Identify key decision-makers
5. **Strategy Planning** — Create your revenue growth roadmap
6. **Implementation** — Execute campaigns and workflows
7. **Measurement** — Track performance and optimize
8. **Continuous Improvement** — Refine based on data insights
